Divisions of the Ministry of Home Affairs

Overview

The Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) operates through specialized divisions that manage internal security, border affairs, disaster response, and administrative coordination. Each division plays a vital role in ensuring peace, stability, and governance across India.

Key Divisions

  • Internal Security Division: Handles law and order, counter-terrorism, and police reforms.
  • Border Management Division: Oversees border security, fencing, and coordination with neighboring countries.
  • Disaster Management Division: Coordinates national disaster response and relief operations.
  • Human Rights Division: Ensures protection of fundamental rights and oversees related commissions.
  • Police Division: Manages central police organizations and modernization of police forces.
  • Judicial Division: Deals with constitutional provisions, legal matters, and Centre–State relations.
  • Finance Division: Handles budgetary allocations and financial planning for MHA programs.
