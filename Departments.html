Departments of the Ministry of Home Affairs

Overview

The Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) functions through several departments, each entrusted with specific responsibilities to ensure internal security, governance, and coordination across India.

Key Departments

  • Department of Border Management: Handles border security, fencing, and coordination with neighboring countries.
  • Department of Internal Security: Focuses on law and order, counter-terrorism, and police reforms.
  • Department of States: Manages Centre–State relations, including constitutional provisions and inter-state matters.
  • Department of Official Language: Promotes the use of Hindi and other official languages in government communication.
  • Department of Jammu, Kashmir and Ladakh Affairs: Oversees governance and development initiatives in these Union Territories.
  • Department of Home: General administration, human rights, and coordination of security agencies.
