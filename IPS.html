GOVERNMENT OF INDIA

Indian Police Service (IPS)

Overview

The Indian Police Service (IPS) is one of the three All India Services of the Government of India. It plays a vital role in maintaining law and order, preventing crime, and ensuring the security of citizens across the nation.

Roles & Responsibilities

  • Maintaining public peace and order
  • Preventing and investigating crimes
  • Border policing and counter-terrorism
  • VIP security and disaster response
  • Leading and managing police forces across states

Training

IPS officers undergo rigorous training at the Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel National Police Academy (SVPNPA), Hyderabad. The training covers law, criminology, leadership, and modern policing techniques.

Organizational Structure

IPS officers serve in key leadership positions in state police forces, central armed police forces, intelligence agencies, and other security organizations.
