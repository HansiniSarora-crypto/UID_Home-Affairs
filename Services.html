GOVERNMENT OF INDIA

MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS

Citizen Services

Overview

The Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) provides a wide range of services to citizens, law enforcement agencies, and state governments. These services are designed to strengthen internal security, promote transparency, and improve governance through digital platforms.

Key Services

  • Passport & Visa Services: Coordination with the Ministry of External Affairs for issuing passports and visas.
  • Police Services: Online portals for FIR registration, police verification, and grievance redressal.
  • Disaster Management Services: Emergency response helplines, relief coordination, and disaster preparedness resources.
  • Border Management Services: Information on border permits, security advisories, and coordination with border states.
  • Human Rights & RTI: Platforms for filing Right to Information requests and human rights complaints.
  • Online Portals: Access to e‑governance portals such as National Database of Arms Licenses, Immigration services, and Central Armed Police Forces recruitment portals.

Government Schemes

The Ministry also implements various schemes to support modernization of police forces, strengthen border infrastructure, and enhance community policing initiatives.

  • Modernisation of Police Forces (MPF) Scheme
  • Border Area Development Programme (BADP)
  • Cyber Crime Prevention against Women & Children (CCPWC)
