About the Ministry of Home Affairs

Our Mission

The Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) is entrusted with the responsibility of ensuring internal security, maintaining law and order, managing borders, and coordinating disaster response across India.

Leadership

  • Union Minister:Shri Amit Shah
  • Home Secretary:Govind Mohan
  • Special Secretary:Praveen Vashista (IPS)

Organizational Structure

The Ministry is organized into divisions and departments, each responsible for specific functions such as border management, police reforms, human rights, and disaster management.
