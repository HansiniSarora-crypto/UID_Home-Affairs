Press Note

Press Release - April 2026

The Ministry of Home Affairs announces new initiatives to strengthen border security and enhance disaster response capabilities. The measures include deployment of advanced surveillance systems, improved coordination with state police forces, and expansion of training programs for emergency response teams.

Citizens are encouraged to participate in awareness campaigns and community outreach programs to foster cooperation between the public and law enforcement agencies.
